# Software Decision Pack

Final decision-support artifact for management review and procurement handoff.

Includes project need, requirements, evaluated products, decision matrix, shortlist/recommendation, verified providers, risks/assumptions, ROI/TCO, business case and RFP references. Every section keeps its source/provenance and missing data is disclosed rather than invented.

Out of scope: purchase requisitions, approvals, purchase orders, contract execution, invoicing, receiving and procurement operations.
