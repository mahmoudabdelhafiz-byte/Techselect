# Workforce Rostering & Shift Scheduling Depth Program

## Objective

Build a specialist software-selection category for operational workforce rostering rather than a generic HR scheduling directory.

The foundation defines **58 buyer-selectable operational criteria** across nine modules, plus the standard category-scoped mobile-access criteria:

1. Demand & Staffing Planning
2. Roster & Shift Generation
3. Compliance, Fatigue & Fairness
4. Operational Workforce Allocation
5. Employee Self-Service
6. Approval, Governance & Audit
7. Integration & Workforce Data
8. Analytics & Operational Control
9. Platform & Operational Scale

The model intentionally includes concerns common in terminals, logistics, manufacturing, energy and other shift-heavy environments: skills/certifications, crews and gangs, operational work areas, equipment qualifications, fatigue/rest rules, overtime, live backfill, intraday reassignment and 24/7 continuity.

## ManpowerIQ relationship disclosure

TechSelectAI is operated by Barmageyat, and ManpowerIQ is a Barmageyat product. This relationship is stored as an explicit product disclosure and shown separately from capability evidence and scoring.

The disclosure must never change Fit Score, Evidence Confidence, ranking position or gap logic. ManpowerIQ must meet the same evidence standard as every other product.

Its foundation evidence is intentionally narrow because the current public Barmageyat page only establishes workforce rostering/manpower planning plus planning, allocation, approval and monitoring across departments, shifts and locations.

## Evidence rule

Unknown is not unsupported.

A product capability is promoted only when the exact scope is supported by current first-party evidence. Generic WFM marketing does not automatically establish a detailed rostering function, deployment model, native mobile platform or operational integration.
